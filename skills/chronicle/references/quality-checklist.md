# Quality checklist

Run it in the `check` step. Every "no" is fixed before delivering.

## Reader

- [ ] Does the first screen answer the reader's question, with the overall confidence when there is one?
- [ ] Could the stated reader act on the summary alone?
- [ ] Are technical terms defined for this reader?

## Fidelity

- [ ] Every finding, figure, citation, locator and confidence label from the content is present and identical.
- [ ] Nothing was added that the content does not say (no new facts, no strengthened or softened claims).
- [ ] Content that did not fit the structure went to an appendix or to the author as a question.
- [ ] Contradictions in the content are flagged, not resolved silently.

## Structure and layout

- [ ] The order follows the reader's needs (answer → support → detail).
- [ ] Headings form a clean hierarchy and carry meaning.
- [ ] Each piece of information is in the right form (prose, list, table, callout, diagram).
- [ ] Every table has a header and an introducing sentence; every diagram a caption and at most 15 nodes.
- [ ] No more than one or two callouts per section.

## Navigation and traceability

- [ ] Every id (F, S, R, C…) and internal link resolves in the document.
- [ ] Long documents have numbered sections or a short contents list.

## Format

- [ ] Only the formats asked for were produced; diagrams are blocks inside the Markdown.
- [ ] Deliverable language is the agreed one; quotes in their original language.
