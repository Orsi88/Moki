# Checklist

Every "no" is fixed (presentation) or returned to the author (content) before delivering.

## Reader and purpose

- [ ] The purpose is clear in the first lines; the reader finds what they need quickly.
- [ ] The order of the information makes sense for this reader; conditions and caveats sit next to what they qualify and are visible.

## Fidelity

- [ ] Facts, numbers, units, dates and periods match the source.
- [ ] Ids (F1, S3, R2, REV-004, item ids) and references are present and identical.
- [ ] Citations keep their attribution and locator; confidence labels unchanged.
- [ ] Fact, hypothesis, proposal, recommendation and approved decision are still distinguished.
- [ ] Nothing was added that the source does not say; a summary kept the essential conditions and limits.

## Structure

- [ ] Headings form a clean hierarchy and help locate the content; terminology is consistent (one term per concept).
- [ ] Tables have a header and an introducing sentence, short cells, a sensible order.
- [ ] Figures have a number and a caption, are mentioned in the text, and represent the content correctly.
- [ ] The model's or skill's mandatory fields, sections, columns and labels are all present and unchanged.
- [ ] Only the formats asked were produced; no helper file next to the deliverable.

## Presentation

Inspect in the format the reader uses:

| Format | Look for |
|---|---|
| Markdown in Moki | Tables readable at the panel's width; no raw HTML or `[!NOTE]` shown as text; diagrams drawn (not an error card); links to headings avoided |
| PDF | Headings not alone at the bottom of a page; tables that continue keep their header; nothing cut at margins; figures whole and legible; header/footer/logo as the template says |
| Word | Opens; styles of the model used; images inside the margins; captions under figures; placeholders all filled |
| Spreadsheet | Opens; sheet and column names exact; header frozen, filters on; no cut text in wrapped columns; formulas and validations of the model intact |
| Tracker text | Renders in the editor's syntax; fields of the template filled; signature at the end |
| Diagrams | Labels readable; few crossings; arrows with a clear origin and target; decisions and paths marked; colours and shapes consistent and not the only carrier of meaning; a caption saying what it shows |

- [ ] Links and references work; the file opens.
- [ ] Every check that could not be done is named in the report.
